Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the HadelFilms TikTok Bot ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our automated video posting service.

2. Information We Collect

This application does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. The bot operates fully automated and only accesses publicly available YouTube video feeds and the TikTok Content Posting API for the purpose of republishing video content.

3. How We Use Information

The only data accessed is:

  • Publicly available YouTube RSS feeds to detect new video uploads.
  • TikTok API tokens for posting content to an authorized TikTok account.

No user data is collected, sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

This application interacts with the following third-party services:

  • YouTube – for RSS feed monitoring (public data only)
  • TikTok – for video publishing via the official Content Posting API

5. Data Retention

No personal data is retained. Temporary video files are deleted automatically after a successful upload.

6. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our TikTok or YouTube channel.
